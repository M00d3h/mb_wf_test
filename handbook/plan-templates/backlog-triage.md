# Backlog triage

**Use for:** keeping `work/backlog/` and `work/issues/` useful. Every couple of weeks, or when they feel noisy.
**Usually needs:** nothing.
**Produces:** tidied trackers, proposed work items, archived records

## Steps

- [ ] Check each open item against the code, not just the tracker. Close anything no longer true, with the reason. Never delete silently.
- [ ] Merge duplicates, keeping the better description. Move issues that have grown into features to the backlog, and small backlog items that are really fixes to issues.
- [ ] Keep blocked items open, with the blocker noted.
- [ ] Group the rest into proposals: a feature or epic, a batch of fixes, quick wins. The engineer decides which become work items.
- [ ] Move closed work items, resolved issues, finished backlog items and old audit reports into `archive/YYYY/` beside them. Nothing is deleted.
- [ ] Close.
