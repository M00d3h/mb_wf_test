# Product brief

**Use for:** a short description of what we're building, for whom, and why, when there isn't one yet. It's what every other item can read before requirements exist.
**Usually needs:** nothing. Any notes, decks or idea documents go in `inputs/`.
**Produces:** `docs/product-brief.md`

## Steps

- [ ] Read the inputs, then interview the engineer: who it's for, the problem it solves, what they do today instead, what success looks like, what's definitely out, deadlines and constraints. Ask a few questions at a time and keep going until you could pitch it back to them. Notes in `brief.md`.
- [ ] State your guess at what kind of product this is ("internal tool for one team", "public multi-tenant web app", "static marketing site") so the engineer can correct it early. Everything later is sized to this.
- [ ] Write `docs/product-brief.md`: one page. Who, problem, what it does, what it doesn't, what success looks like, constraints, open questions. Use the engineer's own words for things.
- [ ] Review in a fresh session: could someone who wasn't in the conversation start the requirements from this alone? What would they have to guess?
- [ ] Close.
